## Full description

Universal PDA is a cross-platform enterprise operations client for warehouse and field workflows.

Connect one mobile app to supported ERP, WMS, OMS, and custom business systems through a normalized connector protocol. The available menus and workflows adapt to the capabilities exposed by the connected system.

Core workflows include:

- Dashboard and operational tasks
- Inventory lookup
- Barcode identification
- Receiving and putaway
- Picking and packing
- Shipping
- Cycle counting
- Inventory movement
- Returns and exception handling
- Multi-warehouse switching
- Offline retry for replay-safe operations
- Camera and industrial PDA barcode input

Universal PDA stores scoped mobile access tokens in the device secure credential store and does not require ERP administrator credentials to be persisted on the device.

The first production connector is NiceC WMS. Additional systems can be integrated through the Universal PDA Connector Protocol.
